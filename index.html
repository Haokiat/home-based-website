Got questions?

We got them answered

What do you mean no added sugar?
With the exception of Chrysanthemum, our ice creams only rely on the natural sweetness of ingredients such as bananas and dates, which also provide dietary fibre.

Consumed in moderation, Chrysanthemum is still diabetic friendly. It contains Allulose which has a minimal impact on blood glucose and insulin for most people.
How are your ice creams high protein?
We use high protein milk on top of protein rich foods like cottage cheese, greek yogurt (not greek inspired!) and tofu made from 100% non-gmo soybeans.

We also use PB Fit instead of peanut butter to maintain a primarily protein dominant macros profile.
How do I place an order?
#1 Place an order on our website — this step will require you to have a Telegram account so we can contact you and make sure your order gets to you.

#2 Make PayNow payment to the QR code displayed, and attach the screenshot showing transaction evidence including amount sent.

#3 You will be guided to Telegram to send us a summary of your order.

#4 Wait for our confirmation message!
How do I get free delivery?
Delivery fee is $15. But if you order 4 pints we will deliver for free!
Why can't I order more than 4?
Coz of capacity constraints... Guys this is a passion project <3
How should I store my ice cream?
Store in tummy, else freeze ASAP!

Ideally, consume within 3 days as we use fresh ingredients, without adding preservatives & binding agents.
What if I want to change my order?
Please let us know through Telegram and we will try our best to accommodate within reason — we cannot un-make ice cream 😭
Why isn't my order confirmed after PayNow?
Confirmation can only occur after we have checked that we have capacity to fulfil your orders — else we will refund you.
Any discounts?
Join our Telegram channel! We have a loyalty program coming soon!
Are the calories listed accurate?
We are a home based business and do not send products for lab testing. Figures were derived from approximates based on ingredients used.

For example, 100g avocado on MyFitnessPal / Ventricle Tracker is ~160 calories.
What happens if I'm late for pick up?
Please let us know in advance so we can move your ice cream back to the freezer.
